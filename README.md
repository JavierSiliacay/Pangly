## 📖 Overview

**Pangly** is an encrypted, privacy-first personal vault paired with an on-device conversational AI assistant.

Instead of scattering photos of driver's licenses across messaging apps, saving passwords in unencrypted notes, or uploading sensitive personal documents to third-party cloud servers, **Pangly keeps all data strictly isolated on your physical device**.

Ask **Pangly** anything in plain language—such as *"When is my next vehicle oil change?"* or *"Show my passport photo"*—and receive instant, verified answers and high-resolution document images in seconds.

## What Makes Pangly Different

### 1. "Ask Pangly" On-Device Intelligence
* Natural language queries to find saved records, renewal dates, logins, and vehicle details instantly.
* Real-time retrieval of original document photos and ID cards without manual searching.
* Sensitive fields remain concealed behind phone-native biometric authentication (Face ID / Fingerprint).

### 2. All-in-One Personal Vault
* **Documents & IDs**: Store driver's licenses, passports, health insurance cards, and badges with automatic expiry countdowns.
* **Credentials & Passwords**: Offline password manager with category organization and one-tap secure copy.
* **Vehicle Logbook**: Track mileage, maintenance history, and upcoming service intervals.
* **Private Notes**: Encrypted scratchpad for sensitive notes and emergency information.
* **Automated Reminders**: Built-in scheduler for renewal deadlines and vehicle maintenance.

### 3. Absolute Privacy by Design
* **Zero Cloud Dependence**: No external servers, no tracking endpoints, and no remote backups.
* **Isolated Sandbox**: Document photos are kept in private sandbox storage, completely hidden from your public photo gallery.
* **Offline Master Recovery Phrase**: Complete vault ownership without relying on third-party accounts or reset links.

---

## Privacy Commitment

1. **No Accounts Required**: No email signup, phone number verification, or server authentication.
2. **Zero Telemetry**: No tracking SDKs, third-party analytics, or background data collection.
3. **Your Device, Your Data**: All encryption, processing, and storage stay exclusively on your hardware.
