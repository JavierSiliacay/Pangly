## 📖 Overview

**Pangly** is an encrypted, privacy-first personal vault powered by an **on-device autonomous action agent**.

Instead of scattering photos of IDs across chat apps, manually typing expense receipts, saving passwords in unencrypted notes, or uploading sensitive personal documents to third-party cloud servers, **Pangly takes real actions and keeps all data strictly isolated on your physical device**.

Ask or tell **Pangly** anything naturally via text or voice—such as *"Log a $25 fuel expense"*, *"Save my passport and remind me 3 months before it expires"*, or *"When is my next vehicle service?"*—and Pangly autonomously organizes records, schedules alerts, and retrieves documents instantly.

## What Makes Pangly Different

### 1. Autonomous "Ask & Act" Action Agent
* **Real On-Device Tool Execution**: Pangly doesn't just chat—it autonomously saves documents, records maintenance & expenses, sets reminder alerts, and organizes notes.
* **🎙️ Voice Message Dictation**: Tap or hold to talk for hands-free voice command execution directly on your device.
* **Instant Document Perception (OCR & Clarity Gating)**: Auto-extracts details from bills, IDs, receipts, and documents with built-in image quality validation.
* **Privacy-Preserving Search**: Sensitive passwords remain concealed behind phone-native biometric authentication (Face ID / Fingerprint).

### 2. All-in-One Personal Vault
* **Documents & IDs**: Store driver's licenses, passports, health insurance cards, and badges with automatic expiry countdowns.
* **Credentials & Passwords**: Offline password manager with category organization and one-tap secure copy.
* **Vehicle Logbook**: Track mileage, maintenance history, and upcoming service intervals.
* **Private Notes**: Encrypted scratchpad for sensitive notes and emergency information.
* **Automated Reminders**: Built-in scheduler for renewal deadlines and vehicle maintenance.

### 3. Absolute Privacy by Design
* **Zero Cloud Dependence**: No external servers, no tracking endpoints, and no remote backups.
* **Isolated Sandbox**: Document photos are kept in private sandbox storage, completely hidden from your public photo gallery.
* **Offline Master Recovery Phrase**: Complete vault ownership without relying on third-party accounts or reset links.
